What I Love Doing

Sports, Outdoors & Tech

From the baseball diamond to freshwater lakes and coding terminals, here is a snapshot of my primary hobbies.

⚾

Baseball & Pitching

Pitching and playing team baseball throughout the year. I focus on pitch mechanics, velocity consistency, bullpen sessions, and team communication on the mound.

Key Focus: Pitching rotation, fastball control, conditioning, and team games.
🎣

Bass & Shore Fishing

Freshwater largemouth bass fishing on local ponds and lakes, as well as saltwater pier casting. I enjoy experimenting with different lures, topwater frogs, and reading water currents.

Favorite Spots: Local ponds, freshwater reservoirs, and coastal night casting.
🌊

Ocean & Coastal Life

Spending time along the shoreline, exploring beaches, watching the surf, and catching night coastal views where the city lights reflect across the ocean waves.

Highlights: Evening shoreline walks, coastal scenery, and peaceful ocean breezes.
💻

Web Development & Coding

Building interactive websites and exploring web design. I enjoy creating semantic HTML layouts, writing modern responsive CSS styling, and programming interactive JavaScript widgets.

Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Node.js / Express, and UI styling.
🥾

Hiking & Trail Exploring

Exploring regional nature reserves, woodland walking trails, and state parks. Getting outside helps recharge focus, stay active, and appreciate nature.

Activities: Trail runs, hill climbs, nature walks, and weekend park hikes.
🐕

Dog Training & Agility

Working on agility hurdles, obedience training, and endurance fetch sessions in the backyard and local parks with Buster, my golden/lab companion.

Routine: Daily fetch drills, leash manners, hurdle jumps, and positive reinforcement.
