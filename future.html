Future Goals: Next 5 Years

My five-year plan is about steady progress in two areas I care about: becoming comfortable speaking Spanish and continuing to grow as a baseball player. Both goals will take regular practice, so I want to keep moving forward one step at a time.

Goal 1: Learn Spanish

I want to practice regularly, build my vocabulary, and become comfortable having conversations in Spanish. I’ll measure progress by what I can understand and say, not just by how many words I memorize.

Goal 2: Make Baseball Team

I want to keep training, improve my skills, and work toward making the high school team. Each season is a chance to keep learning and growing as a baseball player while I work toward that goal.
